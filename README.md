### :cherry_blossom: the girl behind the commits

I'm **Saumya Sharma**, a senior full-stack engineer who turns messy data and complicated workflows into tools people can actually use.

I spent nearly six years at **MathWorks**, building internal developer platforms from the first user conversation to production deployment. My work has included interactive dashboards, HTTP APIs, Go ingestion tools, and the infrastructure keeping them running.

- Built an analytics platform used by **~500 engineers across 50+ teams**.
- Replaced walls of failure records with interactive charts, filters, and collaborative workflows.
- Took legacy Perl tooling into its **Go era**, rolling it out to approximately 500 developers.
- Owned the full stack, including the less photogenic bits: databases, deployments, and production troubleshooting.
